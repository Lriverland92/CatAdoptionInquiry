F2 · Task and Data Plan

Task: A fictional shelter's initial cat adoption inquiry. The form helps staff understand the applicant's preferences and arrange a follow-up. It is not a final adoption application and does not collect real data.

Fields marked required are necessary for a useful initial inquiry. All testing uses fictional information.

Visible label / question Why necessary now? Required? Native control/type Submitted name/value Autocomplete/inputmode Constraint/instruction Sensitivity and minimization decision
Full name Know how to address the person during follow-up. Yes input type="text" full_name name Required; use a fictional name in tests. Basic contact detail; no identity documents requested.
Email address Send a follow-up about available cats. Yes input type="email" email email Required; browser checks email format. Contact information; no phone number or address collected.
Preferred contact time Help staff choose an appropriate time to reply. No select contact_time=morning / afternoon / evening N/A Optional; choose one time period. Preference only; no exact schedule required.
Preferred cat age Help match the inquiry to suitable cats. Yes select cat_age=kitten / adult / senior / any N/A Required; choose one option. Non-sensitive adoption preference.
Interested in a specific cat? Let staff identify the cat if the person has one in mind. No input type="text" cat_name off Optional; leave blank if undecided. Only a cat's name; no extra personal information.
How many cats already live in your home? Understand the existing pet environment. Yes input type="number" current_cats N/A; inputmode="numeric" Required; whole number 0–20, step 1. Household pet count, not personal identity information.
Other pets in your home Help staff consider introductions with other animals. No checkboxes other_pets=dog / small_animal / other N/A Optional; select all that apply, or none. Only pet categories; no detailed household history.
Housing situation Identify whether a follow-up about pet policies may be useful. Yes radio buttons housing=own / rent / other N/A Required; select one. Broad category only; no landlord or financial information.
Are cats permitted where you live? Identify a practical adoption consideration. Yes radio buttons cats_allowed=yes / no / unsure N/A Required; select one. No proof or lease documents collected.
When would you like to adopt? Help staff prioritize follow-up timing. Yes select timeline=soon / one_to_three_months / exploring N/A Required; choose one timeframe. General timing rather than a personal calendar.
Would you like information about introducing a cat to your home? Offer relevant guidance during follow-up. No input type="checkbox" intro_guidance=yes N/A Optional; unchecked means no request. Simple support preference.
Anything else we should know? Allow a short optional clarification about the cat or household. No textarea message N/A Optional; maximum 240 characters. Do not include sensitive details. Length-limited; explicitly discourage private or medical information.
